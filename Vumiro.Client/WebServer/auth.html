Privacy notice and your rights

Vumiro uses your email to sign you in and stores your profile, friends, blocked identities, linked devices and preferences to restore them across devices. Google or Microsoft shares your email and basic profile when you choose that option. Vumiro does not request your contacts, mail or files.

Your private signing identity is encrypted in the app before backup. Other synced account fields can be read by authorized Vumiro infrastructure operators and providers. Screens, audio, chat, room passwords, raw private keys and recovery codes are not uploaded to the account service.

You can export, correct or delete account data in account settings. Local profile data remains on the device. Registration remains unavailable until the project owner publishes the controller identity, privacy contact, provider locations, transfer terms and retention policy in the full PRIVACY.md notice included with Vumiro.

You can connect an account later in settings.
