# Product

Keep the last screen visible during catch-up/reconfiguration and blend into the next decoded image. Capture/receive rates should be visible and target FPS should be honored where resources permit. Participants join one call and can each share without leaving ScreenTask or starting a personal server. A central handshake service exchanges connection messages; media travels between participants.

Success: no empty transition frame in regression checks; three local participants can share concurrently and stop/restart without leaving the room; chosen 60 FPS is requested instead of silently capped at 30. Remote direct connectivity remains dependent on the networks.
